03Analyze — projects in depth

Three problems, worked from question to recommendation.

Global Supply Chain Analytics & Congestion Study

SQLCTEsWindow FunctionsTableauExcel
View repo ↗
Problem

Transport latency was quietly eating into revenue somewhere inside a sprawling, multi-table supply chain dataset — but nobody could point to where.

Approach

Wrote optimised SQL scripts using multi-table joins, CTEs, window functions and aggregations to extract and audit 18,000+ raw transactional rows, then ran cohort and churn analysis in SQL and Excel to isolate the bottlenecks.

Result

An interactive Tableau / Google Sheets dashboard turned shipping-lane efficiency into decision-ready recommendations, projecting the hypothetical revenue loss caused by transport delays.

Explainable Data Pipeline for Clinical Diagnostics

PythonSVMSeabornStreamlitFeature EngineeringFinal Year Project
View repo ↗
Problem

High-dimensional medical and acoustic data is too noisy for a clinician to read directly — the signal for a speech impairment triage was buried in raw waveforms.

Approach

Engineered statistical pipelines for outlier detection, missing-value imputation and automated feature selection; extracted acoustic biomarkers (MFCCs, jitter, shimmer) and mapped correlations with Seaborn and Matplotlib.

Result

An interactive Streamlit screening dashboard built around a custom health-metric index (WPI), converting multidimensional signal into an intuitive, actionable clinical report.

FLARE-HEALTH: AI-Driven IoT-WSN for Flood-Triggered Disease Surveillance

IoT / WSNLSTMRandom ForestPSOTime Series
View Paper ↗ Presentation Certificate ↗
Problem

Flood-prone regions need visibility into contamination risk before it becomes an outbreak — not after, once water and disease surveillance data has already lagged behind reality.

Approach

Co-designed an end-to-end IoT wireless sensor network with biosensors for real-time contamination, water-level, humidity and temperature monitoring; combined Kalman filtering, exponential moving average and LSTM for flood prediction, Random Forest for contamination classification, and Particle Swarm Optimisation for sensor placement.

Result

99.8% classification accuracy for water contamination and a composite Disease Risk Index, computed via ensemble learning and presented at the International Conference on AI-HCSST 2025.
